# 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary personas for the Decision Intelligence Platform (DIP).

Personas represent archetypal users based on expected behaviors, goals and pain points. They guide product decisions, user experience, feature prioritization and future platform evolution.

The personas described here represent the initial target audience for the Travel Intelligence domain and may evolve as additional business domains are introduced.

---

# 2. Persona Overview

| ID | Persona | Primary Goal |
|----|---------|--------------|
| P-001 | Smart Traveler | Plan better trips with less effort |
| P-002 | Business Traveler | Optimize time, cost and convenience |
| P-003 | Travel Optimizer | Maximize value across multiple decision criteria |

---

# 3. Persona P-001 — Smart Traveler

## Profile

Individuals who travel occasionally and want confidence that they are making the best overall decision without spending hours comparing websites.

### Goals

- Find the best travel option quickly.
- Stay within budget.
- Reduce research effort.
- Understand why one option is recommended over another.

### Pain Points

- Too many websites to compare.
- Information changes frequently.
- Difficult to evaluate trade-offs.
- Fear of choosing a poor option.

### Success Criteria

The platform provides a trustworthy recommendation that saves time and simplifies decision-making.

---

# 4. Persona P-002 — Business Traveler

## Profile

Professionals who travel regularly and prioritize efficiency, flexibility and reliability over simply finding the lowest price.

### Goals

- Minimize travel time.
- Reduce itinerary complexity.
- Avoid unnecessary connections.
- Make fast, well-informed decisions.

### Pain Points

- Limited time for research.
- Frequent schedule changes.
- Need to balance cost with convenience.

### Success Criteria

The platform highlights the most efficient travel options based on configurable priorities.

---

# 5. Persona P-003 — Travel Optimizer

## Profile

Experienced travelers who enjoy comparing alternatives and optimizing every aspect of a trip, including flights, hotels, weather, exchange rates and loyalty programs.

### Goals

- Maximize overall trip value.
- Optimize mileage usage.
- Evaluate multiple scenarios.
- Customize recommendation criteria.

### Pain Points

- Manual spreadsheet maintenance.
- Fragmented information.
- Difficult scenario comparison.
- Time-consuming analysis.

### Success Criteria

The platform consolidates all relevant information and generates transparent, configurable recommendations.

---

# 6. Shared Needs

Regardless of profile, all personas expect the platform to:

- Aggregate information from multiple providers.
- Present trustworthy and up-to-date data.
- Explain recommendation results.
- Reduce manual effort.
- Support informed decision-making.

---

# 7. Design Implications

The personas influence several product decisions:

- Prioritize clarity over feature density.
- Make recommendation criteria visible.
- Keep workflows simple.
- Allow advanced configuration without overwhelming new users.
- Ensure recommendations are explainable.

---

# 8. Future Personas

As the platform expands beyond Travel Intelligence, additional personas will be introduced for new domains such as Finance, Insurance, Retail and Healthcare.

The platform architecture should support this evolution without requiring changes to the core decision-making model.
