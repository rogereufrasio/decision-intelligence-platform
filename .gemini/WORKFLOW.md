# Development Workflow

Every implementation must follow this sequence.

## Step 1

Understand the feature request.

## Step 2

Review:

- AGENTS.md
- ADRs
- DIPs
- .ai/context
- .ai/rules

## Step 3

Identify architectural impacts.

## Step 4

Describe the proposed solution before coding.

## Step 5

Implement the solution.

## Step 6

Generate unit tests.

## Step 7

Update documentation when required.

## Step 8

Provide a concise implementation summary.

Never skip documentation updates when architecture or product behavior changes.
