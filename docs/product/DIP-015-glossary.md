# 1. Purpose

This glossary defines the common terminology used throughout the Decision Intelligence Platform documentation.

Its objective is to ensure consistency across Product, Architecture and Engineering artifacts.

---

# 2. Terms

## Alternative

A possible option evaluated during a decision.

---

## Analytics

The process of transforming data into insights.

---

## Business Capability

A reusable business function delivered by the platform.

---

## Canonical Model

A provider-independent representation of business data.

---

## Decision

A business problem that requires evaluating one or more alternatives.

---

## Decision Intelligence

The discipline of supporting decision-making through data, analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

---

## Decision Engine

The platform component responsible for evaluating alternatives and generating recommendations.

---

## Domain

A specific business context supported by the platform.

Example:

Travel Intelligence.

---

## Explainability

The ability to understand how a recommendation was generated.

---

## Provider

A component responsible for integrating with an external system.

---

## Recommendation

The preferred alternative generated by the Decision Engine.

---

## Scoring

The process of assigning numerical values to alternatives according to configured criteria.

---

## Strategy Pattern

A design pattern used to support interchangeable recommendation algorithms.

---

## Travel Intelligence

The first business domain implemented by the Decision Intelligence Platform.

---

# Conclusion

This glossary establishes a shared vocabulary that should be used consistently throughout all project documentation and implementation.
