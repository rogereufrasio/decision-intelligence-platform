# 1. Executive Summary

The Decision Intelligence Platform (DIP) is a modular platform designed to help users make better decisions by consolidating data from multiple sources, applying business rules, analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

Unlike traditional comparison tools that focus on a single domain, the DIP is designed as a generic Decision Intelligence Platform capable of supporting multiple business domains through a modular and extensible architecture.

The first supported domain will be **Travel Intelligence**, allowing users to compare flights, hotels, exchange rates, weather conditions and loyalty programs in a single decision context.

Future domains may include Finance, Insurance, Retail, Healthcare and Supply Chain without requiring architectural redesign.

The project will be developed following professional Product Management, Enterprise Architecture and Software Engineering practices while embracing the productivity gains provided by Vibe Coding.

# 2. Vision

Enable individuals and organizations to make better decisions through trustworthy data, intelligent recommendations and transparent decision-making processes.

---

# 3. Mission

Build an extensible Decision Intelligence Platform capable of transforming fragmented information into actionable recommendations using modern software architecture, analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Decision making often requires consulting multiple systems, comparing inconsistent information and manually evaluating several variables before selecting the best option.

This process is:

- Time consuming
- Error prone
- Highly manual
- Difficult to reproduce
- Poorly documented
- Frequently influenced by incomplete information

Current solutions generally specialize in a single problem and rarely provide transparent explanations about how recommendations are generated.

---

# 5. Opportunity

Recent advances in APIs, cloud-native development, embedded analytics and Generative AI enable the creation of intelligent systems capable of consolidating heterogeneous information and recommending optimal decisions based on user-defined criteria.

The Decision Intelligence Platform aims to bridge this gap by combining:

- Data Integration
- Analytics
- Business Rules
- Artificial Intelligence
- Explainable Recommendations

into a single extensible platform.

---

# 6. Product Vision

The Decision Intelligence Platform is not intended to become another travel comparison website.

Instead, it aims to become a reusable platform capable of supporting decision-making processes across multiple business domains.

Travel Intelligence represents the first implementation of this vision.

---

# 7. Value Proposition

The platform delivers value by:

- Consolidating information from multiple providers
- Reducing manual comparison efforts
- Providing transparent recommendations
- Supporting personalized decision criteria
- Enabling data-driven decisions
- Maintaining full explainability of recommendation results
